Coming Late 2027

Venix NextGen
Conference 2027

A new annual platform for the people shaping what comes next. The conference will bring together professionals, educators, technology leaders, employers, policymakers, researchers, entrepreneurs and emerging talent to explore how new technologies are changing work, learning and society.

Register Your Interest Partnership Enquiry
Why the Conference

A meeting point for the next generation and the institutions shaping its future.

The Venix NextGen Conference will extend VNGI beyond programmes and classrooms. It will create a public platform for serious conversations, practical learning, partnerships and new ideas around emerging technology and human capability.

Future of Work

How emerging technologies are changing careers, organisations, productivity and professional standards.

Education and Skills

How schools, universities and employers can prepare people for rapid technological change.

Responsible Technology

Judgment, verification, ethics, safety and accountability in technology-enabled decisions.

Innovation and Enterprise

New products, businesses and opportunities being created by emerging technologies.

Africa and the Next Generation

How African professionals, institutions and innovators can shape rather than simply consume the next technology cycle.

Current Focus: AI

For 2027, artificial intelligence will be a major part of the conversation while the conference remains open to the broader NextGen agenda.
