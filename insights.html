Evidence & Ideas

Our areas of inquiry

01

AI Literacy & Human Judgment

What it means to understand, verify and responsibly use AI, beyond producing polished outputs.

02

Future of Work & Professional Skills

How job tasks, education pathways and professional standards may change as emerging technologies evolve.

03

Education in the AI Era

Practical approaches to assessment, teacher readiness, academic integrity and student capability.

04

Responsible Technology

Verification, safety, bias, accountability, human oversight and the limits of automated conclusions.

05

Africa & Emerging Technologies

The opportunities, constraints and institutional choices shaping technology readiness across African contexts.

06

Original Research Roadmap

Potential surveys and reports on learner readiness, institutional practice and workplace capability, subject to research design and funding.

Our Approach

How we will publish

01

Ask a meaningful question

Begin with a clear problem and define the limits of the work.

02

Gather credible evidence

Use transparent sources and suitable research methods.

03

Review carefully

Separate findings, interpretation and opinion, and check references.

04

Share what is useful

Publish accessible briefs, practitioner discussions and full methodology where appropriate.
