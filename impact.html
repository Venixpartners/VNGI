We believe impact should be supported by evidence, not invented statistics. This page explains how VNGI will measure learning, programme quality and wider access as verified results become available.

Accountability & Outcomes

What we measure

01

Access & Participation

Number of eligible applicants, enrolled learners, attendance and supported places, reported by programme and period.

02

Completion & Retention

Participation through the full learning pathway and completion of required activities.

03

Knowledge & Judgment

Pre- and post-programme assessments that test understanding, questioning, verification and responsible use.

04

Applied Work

Capstones, practical exercises and examples of work participants can explain and defend.

05

Institutional Reach

Participating schools, employers, universities and community partners, with consent to publish their names.

06

Safeguarding & Feedback

Participant feedback, concerns, corrective actions and safe handling of information, especially for minors.

Our Approach

Our reporting commitments

01

Verify before publishing

We do not publish participant counts or outcomes without supporting records.

02

Respect consent

Learner stories, portraits and project samples are only shared with appropriate permission.

03

Show context

Reports specify time period, population, method and important limitations.

04

Improve openly

We use programme feedback and assessment findings to improve future cohorts.
