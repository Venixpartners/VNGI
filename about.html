Venix NextGen Initiative

Why Venix NextGen

A NextGen initiative, not an AI-only initiative.

Artificial intelligence is our current flagship focus because it is already reshaping work, learning and decision-making. VNGI is designed to stay relevant as the technology landscape changes.

We are building the knowledge, judgment and adaptability people need to work confidently with emerging technologies today and whatever comes next.

Our Vision

Future-ready people who can lead anywhere.

To build a generation of African professionals who can confidently understand, adapt to and shape the technologies defining the future.

Our Mission

Knowledge, judgment and responsible application.

To equip young people and professionals with the knowledge, judgment, practical skills and ethical awareness needed to use emerging technologies responsibly and effectively through hands-on learning, assessment, research, community and institutional partnerships.

Current AI Objective

Venix NextGen is building the next generation of AI-literate professionals who can use, question, verify, train and apply artificial intelligence responsibly.
