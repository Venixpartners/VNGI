Privacy Policy

How the Venix NextGen Initiative collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Effective Date
23 November 2026 · Version 1.1
Related Documents

1. Introduction

The Venix NextGen Initiative ("Venix NextGen", "the Initiative", "we", "us" or "our") is an AI education and empowerment programme for young Nigerians. It is the social impact arm of Venix Partners Limited (RC 1907456) and is structured as a company limited by guarantee under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria. Our registered office is at 10th Floor, Standard Chartered Building, 142 Ahmadu Bello Way, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect your personal data when you visit our website at venixnextgen.org (the "Website"), apply to join one of our cohorts, take part in our programmes, mentor or volunteer with us, partner with us, or otherwise interact with us. It also explains your rights and how you can exercise them.

We take your privacy seriously. Many of the people we serve are young, and some are under the age of 18, so we hold ourselves to a high standard of care in how we handle personal data. This Policy has been prepared to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA"), the regulations and directives issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission ("NDPC"), and, where applicable to individuals located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the UK GDPR.

By using the Website or providing your personal data to us, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Website or provide your personal data to us.

2. Who is responsible for your data

For the purposes of the NDPA, the Venix NextGen Initiative is the data controller of the personal data described in this Policy. This means we decide why and how your personal data is processed. Where Venix Partners Limited provides administrative, technical or governance support to the Initiative, it does so under arrangements that require it to protect your data to the same standard set out in this Policy.

You can contact us about anything in this Policy at programme@venixnextgen.org or by writing to the address above. Please mark your correspondence "Data Protection".

3. The personal data we collect

We only collect personal data that we genuinely need to run the Initiative well. The data we collect depends on how you interact with us.

3.1 When you apply to join a cohort

Our online application form collects the following information:

  • Your first name and last name;
  • Your email address and phone number;
  • Your age range (14 to 17, 18 to 25, or 26 to 35);
  • Your address, state of residence and country;
  • If you are aged 14 to 17, the name, phone number and, optionally, the email address of your parent or guardian, and confirmation that they know about and agree to your application;
  • A record that you accepted this Privacy Policy, and the date and page from which you applied.

Application forms submitted through the Website are processed using Google Apps Script and stored in Google Workspace, a service provided by Google. Access is limited to the VNGI staff who manage applications.

If your application progresses, we may ask for additional information needed to place you in a cohort, such as your educational status, your access to a device and internet connection, and, where you are under 18, the name and contact details of your parent or guardian together with their consent.

3.2 When you take part in a programme

  • Attendance and participation records for live sessions and workshops;
  • Assignments, weekly challenge submissions and capstone project work;
  • Messages you send in official programme channels, such as moderated WhatsApp groups used for submissions and announcements;
  • Mentoring session records, including scheduling details and brief session notes;
  • Feedback, survey responses and evaluation data;
  • Certificate records, including your name, cohort, completion status and a verification identifier.

3.3 Photography, video and testimonials

With your consent, and with the consent of a parent or guardian where you are under 18, we may collect photographs, video recordings and testimonials from sessions and events for reporting, communications and social media. Consent for this is always sought separately and is never a condition of taking part in the programme. You can decline or withdraw this consent at any time without affecting your place in a cohort.

3.4 When you mentor, facilitate, volunteer or partner with us

We collect the information reasonably needed to work with you, which may include your name, contact details, professional background, references, identification for vetting purposes (particularly where your role involves contact with minors), bank details for the payment of any agreed honoraria, and records of the work you do with us.

3.5 When you visit the Website

Like most websites, we automatically collect limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit and how long you spend on them. Some of this is collected through cookies and similar technologies, which are explained in our separate Cookie Policy.

3.6 Data we do not seek

We do not ask for sensitive personal data (such as data revealing religious beliefs, ethnic origin, health status or political opinions) through the application form, and we ask you not to include it in your submissions. If a safeguarding matter requires us to record sensitive information in order to protect a participant, we do so under our Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy, on a strict need to know basis, and in line with the NDPA rules on sensitive personal data.

4. How we collect your data

  • Directly from you, when you complete the application form, contact us by email, take part in sessions, submit work, complete surveys or communicate with us in official channels;
  • From your parent or guardian, where you are under 18 and they provide information or consent on your behalf;
  • From schools and partner organisations, where they refer you to the programme, and only with appropriate notice to you;
  • Automatically, through the Website as described in the Cookie Policy.

5. Why we use your data and our lawful bases

Under the NDPA we must have a lawful basis for every use of your personal data. The table of purposes below sets out what we do and why the law permits it.

5.1 Running the programme

We use application data to assess applications, communicate about next steps, place participants in cohorts, deliver training, mentoring and the capstone project, track attendance and progress, and issue certificates. Lawful basis: performance of a contract with you (the participation arrangement you enter into when you accept a place), and our legitimate interest in running an organised, credible education programme.

5.2 Communicating with you

We use your contact details to respond to enquiries, send programme information, schedules, reminders and materials, and provide alumni community updates. Lawful basis: performance of a contract and legitimate interests. Where we send communications that are not necessary for the programme itself, such as newsletters, we rely on your consent, and every such message includes a simple way to opt out.

5.3 Safeguarding

We use personal data to keep participants safe, including vetting the adults who work with young people, recording and acting on safeguarding concerns, and reporting to the authorities where the law requires it. Lawful basis: legal obligation, protection of vital interests, and our legitimate interest in protecting the young people in our care.

5.4 Monitoring, evaluation and reporting

We analyse participation and outcome data to understand what works, improve the curriculum and report to our board, partners and funders. Reports shared outside the Initiative use aggregated and anonymised information; we do not hand over identifiable participant lists to funders unless we have told you and have a lawful basis to do so. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.

5.5 Communications and publicity

Where you have given consent, we use photographs, recordings and testimonials in our reports, on the Website and on social media. Lawful basis: consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

5.6 Legal and administrative purposes

We use data to keep proper corporate, financial and governance records, comply with our obligations under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 and tax law, respond to lawful requests from regulators, and establish or defend legal claims. Lawful basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests.

5.7 Website operation and security

We use technical data to operate, secure and improve the Website, prevent abuse and diagnose problems. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, and consent where cookies that are not strictly necessary are used.

6. Children and young people

Our programme is open to applicants from the age of 14. Under Nigerian law, including the Child Rights Act 2003, a child is a person under the age of 18, and the NDPA requires specific protections when processing a child's personal data. We apply the following rules:

  • Where an applicant indicates that they are under 18, we obtain the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian before enrolling them, and we take reasonable steps, proportionate to available technology, to verify age and the identity of the consenting adult;
  • We collect the minimum data necessary from participants under 18 and apply heightened access restrictions to it;
  • We never publish the image, full name or work of a participant under 18 without the documented consent of a parent or guardian;
  • We present privacy information to young participants in clear, plain language they can understand;
  • All adults who interact with participants under 18 are vetted and bound by our Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy;
  • A parent or guardian may contact us at any time at programme@venixnextgen.org to review, correct or request the deletion of their child's data, or to withdraw consent.

If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 18 without the required parental consent, we will delete it or obtain the missing consent without delay.

7. Who we share your data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. We share personal data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Facilitators, mentors and programme staff, who receive only the information they need to deliver sessions and mentoring, and who are bound by confidentiality and safeguarding obligations;
  • Venix Partners Limited, where it provides governance, administrative or technical support to the Initiative under arrangements that protect your data;
  • Service providers, such as website hosting (Vercel), application form processing and storage (Google Workspace), email, video conferencing, messaging and cloud storage providers, who process data on our documented instructions and under contracts that meet the requirements of the NDPA;
  • Partners and funders, who receive aggregated, anonymised reporting only, unless we have informed you otherwise and have a lawful basis for sharing identifiable data;
  • Schools and referring organisations, limited to confirming the participation and progress of students they referred, where this has been made clear to you;
  • Regulators, law enforcement and courts, where disclosure is required by law, or is necessary to protect a child, to protect our rights, or to respond to a lawful demand;
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants and auditors, under duties of confidentiality.

8. International transfers

Some of our service providers, such as email, cloud storage and video conferencing platforms, store data on servers outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria, we comply with Part VIII of the NDPA. This means we transfer data only where the destination provides an adequate level of protection as recognised by the NDPC, or where appropriate safeguards are in place, such as binding contractual clauses that impose data protection obligations on the recipient, or where a specific exception under the NDPA applies, such as your explicit consent after being informed of the risks. For individuals protected by the EU or UK GDPR, we apply the equivalent transfer safeguards required by those laws.

9. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this Policy, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised. Our standard retention periods are:

  • Unsuccessful applications: up to 12 months after the relevant cohort closes, so that we can consider you for future cohorts, unless you ask us to delete your data sooner;
  • Participant programme records: for the duration of the programme and up to 3 years afterwards, for alumni engagement, evaluation and reporting;
  • Certificate verification records (name, cohort, certificate identifier): retained for as long as the certificate may need to be verified, which supports the long term value of your certificate;
  • Safeguarding records: retained in line with our Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy and applicable legal guidance, which may require longer retention;
  • Financial and governance records: at least 6 years, as required by Nigerian company and tax law;
  • Consent records for photography and media: for as long as the relevant material remains in use, plus a reasonable period to evidence the consent.

10. How we protect your data

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, as required by the NDPA. These include access controls so that only authorised people can see personal data, password protection and, where available, multi factor authentication on our systems, encryption in transit for data submitted through the Website, restricted and supervised use of messaging groups, staff and volunteer confidentiality obligations, and periodic review of who has access to what. No system is completely secure, but if we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the NDPC within the timeframe required by law and, where the risk to you is high, we will notify you directly.

11. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the following rights over your personal data. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian may exercise these rights on your behalf.

  • Access: to ask whether we hold personal data about you and to receive a copy of it;
  • Correction: to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • Deletion: to ask us to delete your data where there is no good reason for us to keep it;
  • Restriction: to ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances;
  • Objection: to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to object at any time to direct marketing;
  • Portability: to receive the data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine readable format;
  • Withdrawal of consent: to withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal;
  • Complaint: to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe we have handled your data unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, email programme@venixnextgen.org with the subject line "Data Rights Request". We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We respond to requests within the timelines set by the NDPA, and we do not charge a fee unless the law permits one for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests. We will never treat you less favourably for exercising your rights.

12. Marketing and communications preferences

We only send you marketing style communications, such as newsletters or invitations unrelated to a programme you are enrolled in, where you have opted in. Every such message includes an unsubscribe option, and you can also opt out at any time by emailing programme@venixnextgen.org. Operational messages, such as session schedules, assignment reminders and safeguarding notices, are part of running the programme and are sent for as long as you are a participant.

13. Cookies and third party links

Optional Meta Pixel: If you accept marketing tracking, our website loads the Meta Pixel (ID 29306401602343905) to measure page views and assess outreach performance. Meta Platforms may receive your browser, device, network and visit information and process it under its own privacy terms. The Pixel is not loaded if you reject marketing tracking. You can change the setting using the Cookie settings control. This optional technology is separate from programme applications.

Our use of cookies and similar technologies is explained in our Cookie Policy, which forms part of this Policy. The Website contains links to third party sites and platforms, including our social media pages on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, and the programme makes use of third party AI tools for learning purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing them with personal data.

14. Automated decision making

We do not make decisions about applicants or participants based solely on automated processing. Applications are reviewed by people. Where we teach with or about AI tools, participant work may be discussed with the help of such tools during training, but no admission, progression or certification decision is made without human review.

15. Changes to this Policy

We review this Policy at least once a year and whenever our practices or the law change. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above, publish the new version on the Website, and, where the change significantly affects current participants, notify you through our official channels. Earlier versions are available on request.

16. How to contact us and complain

Questions, requests and complaints about this Policy or our handling of personal data should be sent to programme@venixnextgen.org or by post to Venix NextGen Initiative, 10th Floor, Standard Chartered Building, 142 Ahmadu Bello Way, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 5 working days and to resolve them within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Use, Cookie Policy, Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy, and Participant Code of Conduct.
