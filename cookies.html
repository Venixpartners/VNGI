Cookie Policy

How the Venix NextGen website uses cookies and similar technologies.

Effective Date
16 July 2026 · Version 1.0

1. About this Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the Venix NextGen Initiative ("we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on venixnextgen.org (the "Website"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which describes how we handle personal data more generally. This Policy is designed to meet the transparency and consent requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the guidance of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and, for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the EU and UK GDPR and applicable ePrivacy rules.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your computer or mobile device when you visit. They allow the website to recognise your device, remember your preferences and understand how the site is being used. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage and scripts, and in this Policy we refer to all of them as "cookies".

Cookies may be "session" cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or "persistent" cookies, which remain on your device for a set period. They may be "first party" cookies, set by us, or "third party" cookies, set by other organisations whose services appear on the Website.

3. The cookies and tracking we use

3.1 Essential website storage

Essential functionality helps visitors navigate the Website, submit forms and retain privacy preferences. We record your cookie decision in your browser's local storage under the key vngi_cookie_preferences_v1.

3.2 Optional marketing tracking: Meta Pixel

With your explicit permission, we load the Meta Pixel (ID 29306401602343905), operated by Meta Platforms, to measure website page views and understand the performance of our outreach and advertising. When enabled, Meta may receive identifiers, IP address, browser information, page URL and other technical information and may use cookies or similar technologies. Meta's own privacy and retention practices also apply. We do not load this Pixel before you accept marketing tracking.

3.3 Other third-party content

Links to external platforms may take you to third-party websites with their own privacy practices. The VNGI Lab is a separate application and may have its own privacy controls.

4. Your choice

On your first visit, you can select Accept marketing or Reject marketing. Marketing tracking is off unless you accept it. Rejecting marketing tracking does not prevent access to the Website or its application form.

5. Managing or withdrawing consent

Use the Cookie settings button on the Website to change your decision. If you withdraw consent after accepting, marketing tracking will not load on future page visits. Requests already sent to Meta cannot be withdrawn retrospectively through this button. You can also clear existing Meta cookies through your browser and contact Meta about your rights.

6. How long preferences last

We store your consent decision in your browser's local storage until you change or clear it. You can reopen the Cookie settings at any time.

7. Cookies and personal data

Some cookie data, such as IP addresses and device identifiers, can be personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Where that is the case, the data is handled in line with our Privacy Policy, including its provisions on security, retention, international transfers and your rights. You can exercise your data protection rights, including the right to object and the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, as described in the Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to this Policy

We will update this Policy when our use of cookies changes or when the law requires. The current version, with its effective date, will always be available on the Website. If we introduce cookies that require consent, we will ask for your consent before setting them.

9. Contact

Questions about this Policy or our use of cookies can be sent to programme@venixnextgen.org or by post to Venix NextGen Initiative, 10th Floor, Standard Chartered Building, 142 Ahmadu Bello Way, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
